TRUST ME BRO: LAST DELIVERY — v2.0

1) Extract the ZIP.
2) Open index.html in Chrome/Edge, or upload the folder as an HTML5 build.
3) Pick a worker. The original soundtrack begins on the first allowed user gesture.

GOAL
One final package remains at 23:57. Carry it through 31 warehouse sectors, scan one clearance stamp in every sector, deliver it to DOCK 31, then clock out.

CONTROLS
Desktop: A/D or arrows = move, Space/W/Up = jump, R = restart.
Mobile: left joystick + right jump button.
