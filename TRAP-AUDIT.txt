TRUST ME BRO: LAST DELIVERY — TRAP & FLOW AUDIT
Build: v2.0

CORE ROUTE
- 31 sectors, 1 playable route segment per sector.
- Goal: carry one final package to DOCK 31, scan one clearance stamp per sector, clock out.
- All sectors use the warehouse/depot visual family; unrelated sea/moon/mars backdrops are removed from the route.

REMOVED AS UNRELATED / PROCEDURAL NOISE
- coinBite procedural spike
- rearBite procedural spike
- floorPop procedural spike
- sweeper red moving hazard
- exitDrop procedural ceiling block
- hunter red chase spike
- lastStep procedural spike
- upgrade-reactive counter traps (oil/cloud hand)
- fish gap hazards
- procedural fan hazards
- procedural launch pads

WHY REMOVED
These systems could activate because of a procedural pattern rather than because the room/route visually communicated them. That created deaths that looked random: hazards appearing above, behind, or beside a normal walking line. Last Delivery now uses scene-authored traps only.
