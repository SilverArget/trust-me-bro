Privacy Policy — Trust Me Bro

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Trust Me Bro is a single-player arcade game published by Boru Gaming. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or device data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting, and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress and your local high score list in your device's storage. This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not readable by us or by any third party;
  • contains no personal information — only level progress and scores;
  • is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions

The app declares a single Android permission, INTERNET, which is required by the underlying app framework. The game itself is fully playable offline and does not need a network connection.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties, because no data is collected in the first place.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
