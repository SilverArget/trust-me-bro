Privacy Policy — Trust Me Bro

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Trust Me Bro is a single-player arcade game published by Boru Gaming. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

The game itself does not ask for, store, or send any personal information. There is no account and no sign-in. The Android version shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads appear only when you tap "Watch ad · +coins" on the results screen; the game never shows an ad on its own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch an ad, Google AdMob may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's Privacy options. See how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress and your local high score list in your device's storage. This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not readable by us or by any third party;
  • contains no personal information — only level progress and scores;
  • is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions

INTERNET (app framework and ads) and AD_ID (advertising ID, used only by the ad SDK). The game is fully playable offline; ads need a connection.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

We do not sell data. Google AdMob processes the advertising data described above under Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
